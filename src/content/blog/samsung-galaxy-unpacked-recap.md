I watched Unpacked live yesterday and now I can't decide between two phones — here's why. Let's quickly discuss everything that has been released. Three phones, two watches, and smart glasses?! This time around the event took place in London and I must admit the time difference threw me off a little. I was so excited to see the new phones — I'd been counting down the days and following the leaks. I was locked in!

## Z Fold8 Ultra

The Samsung Z Fold8 Ultra is the true successor to the Z Fold7 — it shares a lot of the same specs but has a much better battery and hinge. If you have the Fold7, this upgrade might not mean much to you, but coming from the Fold6, the improvements are massive and I think we finally have a foldable device people can pick up and use for the long haul.

**Colors:** Violet Shadow · Graphite · Cream · Green Shadow (Samsung Online Exclusive)

**Price:** Starts at $2,599 CAD ($2,099 USD) for the 256GB option

## Z Fold8

The Samsung Z Fold8 is the phone getting most of the attention, and that's because it's in this new form factor that I think will attract a lot of people who want to try foldables but are turned off by the higher price tag on the Ultra. This form is great for consuming media, even as an e-reader replacement. Its smaller size also makes it more pocketable and easier to hold. I really hope this is the direction we continue to go in terms of size, but the cameras aren't doing it for me personally.

**Colors:** Lavender · Graphite · Cream · Pistachio (Samsung Online Exclusive)

**Price:** Starts at $2,399 CAD ($1,899 USD) for the 256GB option

## Z Flip8

The Samsung Z Flip8 is pretty much the same. Nothing major has changed — sure, the size and weight are slightly different, but if you already own a Z Flip7, you can definitely skip upgrading. Samsung spent more time on One UI 9 features instead, and how it helps make the Flip8 front cover more useful. Since this is a software update, hopefully it trickles down to older models as well.

**Colors:** Pink · Graphite · Cream · Mint (Samsung Online Exclusive)

**Price:** Starts at $1,549 CAD ($1,199 USD) for the 256GB option

## Watch9 & Watch Ultra2

The same goes for the Samsung Galaxy Watch9 — not much changes here, but some cool new bands are available. The Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2 was also announced, and as someone with small wrists, this is a product I never really follow. But if you were a fan of the original Ultra, this new one is lighter and has a brighter display. It's also certified for diving now if that's what you're into — but this watch definitely isn't for me.

**Watch9 colors:** Cream (40mm) · Graphite (40mm & 44mm) · Silver (44mm)
**Ultra2 colors:** Titanium Silver · Titanium Gray

**Price:** Watch9 starts at $519–$559 CAD ($379–$409 USD); Ultra2 starts at $949 CAD ($699 USD)

## Smart Glasses

And then there's the wildcard of the event: Samsung's new smart glasses. This is Samsung's first real swing at the space, and I'd heard the rumours but honestly forgot about them until they showed up on stage. They're clearly aimed at competing with the Meta Ray-Bans, and I'm curious to see others' opinions on this first. I also don't think this will be coming to Canada any time soon, unfortunately.

## My Wishlist

If you've been following me for a while, you know I originally had the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold6 and quickly traded in for the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra, which is what I currently have now. The Z Fold6 was a great phone, but there was a list of things that annoyed me that I couldn't get over:

- Narrow front display
- Battery
- Qi2 charging
- Phone case selection
- Cameras
- Slow charging, etc.

For the most part these have all been corrected, except for built-in Qi2 charging. I don't know why we aren't seeing this yet from Samsung, but I guess the magnet cases are just going to have to do.

Both the Fold8 and the Fold8 Ultra are very appealing to me. The size and overall form factor of the Fold8 is exactly what I want — but lacking proper cameras will annoy me more in the long run, so I think I'm leaning towards the Fold8 Ultra. I usually upgrade my phone yearly, but since things have been slowing down, I find myself holding onto my devices longer these days. The Z Fold8 Ultra will age better in my opinion compared to the Z Fold8. I've been told to skip over the first version of a product — I know in the next couple of years they'll master the Z Fold8 form just like they have with the Ultra, and maybe then I'll give it a try.

Keep in mind when watching phone reviews that a lot of creators get gifted their devices to review, and they aren't always thinking long-term about them. An average consumer like myself is paying for this phone with my own hard-earned money, so it's important I do my own research too when upgrading these days — these aren't cheap! Fun fact: the Z Fold8 Ultra 1TB is the most expensive phone for sale in Canada right now, at a whopping $3,549.

I also currently have the Tab S11, which I love, but its size makes it uncomfortable to hold for longer periods of time. Ideally Samsung would release an iPad mini competitor, but I don't know when that will happen — so I'm hoping the Z Fold8 Ultra can help fill that void. I also enjoy multitasking on my phone more than consuming media (and doomscrolling), which the Z Fold8 Ultra handles better than the Z Fold8.

But after all that back and forth... I just keep going back to the Fold8! The size just looks too good. So I'm still not 100% sold on which device I'll be picking up.
