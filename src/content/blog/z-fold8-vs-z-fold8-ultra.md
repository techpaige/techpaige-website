If you read my [Unpacked recap](/blog/samsung-galaxy-unpacked-recap), you know I couldn't decide between the Z Fold 8 and the Z Fold 8 Ultra. I bought the Ultra first, but doubt crept in fast, so I picked up the regular Fold 8 too and lived with both side by side. Ultimately I kept the Fold 8 and returned the Ultra just before the window closed. Here's why.

## The Ultra looked like the obvious upgrade on paper

It's hard to argue with the spec sheet. The Ultra gets a 200MP main camera plus a 3x optical telephoto, a bigger 8" inner display and 6.5" cover screen, and a slightly larger battery. If cameras and screen real estate were the only things that mattered, it would win outright.

## Why I sent the Ultra back

That folded width row is a big part of the story. The Ultra is narrower when closed, and on paper that sounds like it should feel more refined, but in the hand it just felt cramped. Every time I picked it back up I couldn't get over how narrow it was. It never felt as natural to hold as the regular Fold 8.

It wasn't just the folded width either. Open the Ultra up to its main display and I still ran into the same problem from a different angle. Split two apps side by side to multitask and you end up with two narrow windows looking back at you instead of two usable ones.

The Fold 8, meanwhile, is just *cute*. I might even call it the foldable for the girls. It's so easy to hold, genuinely lightweight, and I have not been able to put it down since I got it. I'm obsessed. Customization with Good Lock has felt completely unlimited too, which matters more to my day to day than a longer zoom range does.

I'm so glad I went with my gut on this one. I almost talked myself into keeping the Ultra for the cameras, and I would have been talking myself out of the phone I actually enjoy using.

## Side by side

![Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra held side by side, screens on](/images/blog/z-fold8-vs-ultra-hero.jpg)

You can see the width difference the second you hold them side by side. The Ultra's cover screen is bigger, but the Fold 8 is the one that feels right in hand.

![Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra camera modules compared](/images/blog/z-fold8-vs-ultra-cameras.jpg)

The camera bump is the biggest visible difference between the two: a 200MP main camera plus a telephoto lens on the Ultra, versus the simpler dual-camera setup on the Fold 8. It's the one spec where the Ultra genuinely pulls ahead.

![Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra side profiles compared, showing the thickness and width difference](/images/blog/z-fold8-vs-ultra-profile.jpg)

This is the shot that sums up the whole decision for me. The Ultra folds down thinner, but noticeably narrower too, and that's the part that never stopped bothering me.

## So which one should you get?

If the camera system and the bigger screens are non-negotiable for you, the Ultra is still a genuinely great phone. It just wasn't right for me. But if you want something that feels light, easy to hold one-handed, and doesn't feel like a brick in your bag, the regular Z Fold 8 is the one I'd point you toward. It's the one I actually reach for.
