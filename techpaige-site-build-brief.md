**Include on the Gear page now:**

### Phone & Wearables
- **Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra** — My daily driver, hands down. The battery holds up all day, the titanium build feels great in hand, and the cameras are unmatched. I'll probably upgrade eventually, but honestly? I don't need to — to get the best of the best, you have to go big.
- **Samsung Galaxy Watch8** — My go-to for tracking workouts, whether that's yoga, cardio, or strength days.
- **Samsung Galaxy Ring** — Everything the watch doesn't cover. Way more comfortable long-term, incredible battery life, and it's helped me disconnect from notifications a little more.
- **Google Pixel 10** — My dedicated business phone, keeping work email and social separate from my personal device. Keeping that line is a big part of staying on top of my digital wellbeing.

### Tablets & eReaders
- **Samsung Galaxy Tab S11** — I keep falling more in love with this one. The screen size and the included S Pen make it perfect for media, digital planning, and bullet journaling — plus the occasional game, and yes, it doubles as a baby monitor. It's basically replacing my laptop for lighter work.
- **Kindle Paperwhite** — Still the best device for comfortable, long-stretch reading, even if I'm not reaching for it as much lately — I'm trying to carry fewer devices day to day.

### Computers
- **MacBook Pro 14" (M4)** — My first Apple product in decades, and it's a beast. M4 Pro chip, 24GB unified memory, 1TB storage — specced for long work sessions, video editing, and bigger projects, especially connected to my desk and monitor setup. Battery life on the go is amazing too. My most expensive piece of tech, and worth every penny.

### Audio
- **Samsung Galaxy Buds3 Pro** — Work seamlessly across my Samsung devices... when I remember to charge them.
- **Sony WH-1000XM4** — My go-to for editing sessions and workouts. (Full honesty: lately my basic USB-C wired headphones get more use when I'm out — no shame in simple sometimes.)
