People ask about my desk setup more than almost anything else I own, so here's the full breakdown — no sponsorships, just the stuff I paid for and kept.

## Branch Standing Desk

The foundation of the whole setup. I go back and forth between sitting and standing a few times a day, and it's genuinely changed how I feel by the end of a long editing session.

## Samsung 32" S80A Monitor

My main screen for everything — writing, editing, research tabs I definitely have too many of. The size makes a real difference when I'm working from the MacBook Pro instead of the laptop screen alone.

## Logitech MX Master 3S

My mouse of choice, hands down. The scroll wheel alone is worth it if you're moving through long documents or timelines all day.

## Logitech MX Keys Mini

Compact, quiet enough for early mornings before the house wakes up, and it pairs across my devices without a fuss.

## Logitech Brio 301

My webcam for calls and any on-camera clips I need without pulling out the full camera setup.

## Logitech Z207 Computer Speakers

Simple, does the job, doesn't take up much desk space — exactly what I wanted here so the desk doesn't feel cluttered.
