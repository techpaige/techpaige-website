My phone's home screen has changed a hundred times, but these ten apps have earned a permanent spot no matter what device I'm carrying.

## 1. TickTick

My source of truth for tasks and content planning — everything from blog post deadlines to reminders lives here first.

## 2. Notion

Where TechPaige actually runs behind the scenes — my content hub and business workspace.

## 3. Tiimo

My daily visual time-blocking app, for when a plain task list isn't enough to actually get me moving.

## 4. Day One

Private, encrypted journaling — my end-of-day space to process before it becomes anything public.

## 5. Hevy

Strength tracking, logged set by set. Simple enough that I actually keep up with it.

## 6. Samsung Health

Steps and sleep tracking, synced with my Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Ring.

## 7. Samsung Gallery

My first stop for quick photo edits — the automatic angle and perspective correction alone saves me so much time.

## 8. Adobe Lightroom

For the photos that need more than a quick fix.

## 9. Expert RAW

Getting more control out of my phone's camera when the default app isn't cutting it.

## 10. Outlook

Business email, kept firmly separate from my personal inbox.
