It's hard to find a good case for a foldable still, since some brands still consider them too niche. The pickings are slim, and the quality can vary even more so. I don't want to have to worry about throwing my phone in my bag or on the floor at the gym. So when Benks reached out, I said yes mostly because I was curious whether a case brand could actually keep up with a foldable lifestyle. A few months and two phones later, here's everything I've tried.

Quick disclosure: Benks has sent me cases as part of an ongoing partnership, and this post includes affiliate links and a discount code. I only write up gear I've actually used, and I'll always tell you when something didn't work for me too.

## What I tested first, on the Z Fold 8 Ultra

Before I switched phones, Benks sent two cases for the Z Fold 8 Ultra: the ArmorGrid Pro Grand Combo in black, and the Crystal Grand Combo, which both came with the magnetic hinge case, a front frame, and a screen protector all together.

The ArmorGrid Pro was the one that actually changed how I felt about carrying the Ultra day to day. It has enough texture to grip without being bulky, and the hinge protection held up through a few drops I'd rather not admit to. The Crystal Grand Combo was the one I reached for when I wanted the phone to still look like itself. Clear cases usually stress me out because they yellow or crack at the hinge fast, but this one has held up so far, granted not much time has passed.

When I ended up returning the Ultra for the regular Fold 8, it wasn't because of anything to do with the cases. That decision was all about the phone itself.

## What's on the Z Fold 8 now

Once I settled on the Fold 8, Benks put together a new set to match: the Grand Combo Crystal Case, the Grand Combo ArmorGrid Pro in Obsidian Black Kevlar, and an ArmorPop Phone Grip & Stand.

The ArmorGrid Pro Kevlar case is the one I've actually been living with. It's the same idea as the Ultra version, tough where the hinge needs it, textured enough to hold onto one-handed, but in the Kevlar finish instead of the plain black, which reads a lot more premium in person than it sounds on paper.

The Crystal Case is still in the box waiting on me. Benks had it temporarily out of stock, so I chose to wait for the real thing instead of taking a substitute color. Worth it, since the clear case is the one I want most for showing off the phone itself.

The grip stand might be the most underrated piece of this whole set. Foldables genuinely need something to hold onto, since the extra width makes one-handed use trickier than a regular phone, and having a stand built in means I can prop it up hands-free for video calls or watching something in bed without hunting for a stand separately.
